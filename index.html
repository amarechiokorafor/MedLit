Understanding your own health shouldn’t be a privilege.

MedLit is a student-led nonprofit that makes health information understandable — free workshops and free plain-language guides.

Get involved See our guides

The problem

Every day, people struggle with prescription labels, lab results, and insurance letters. The print is small, the words are hard to understand, and the person who could explain it has already moved on to the next patient. So people guess. And guessing about your own health is a risk no one should have to take.

What we do

  • Free workshops

    45-minute interactive sessions at senior communities, libraries, and community organizations, covering how to read prescription labels, lab results, and insurance letters.

  • Free guides

    One-page plain-language guides in English and Spanish, reviewed by a licensed healthcare professional before release.

  • Measured results

    We test comprehension before and after every workshop, so we can show people actually learned something.

Guides
