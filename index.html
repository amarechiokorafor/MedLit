The problem

Every day, people struggle with prescription labels, lab results, and insurance letters. The print is small, the words are hard to understand, and the person who could explain it has already moved on to the next patient.

So people guess. And guessing about your own health is a risk no one should have to take.

How we check it

We check the work, and we measure it.

  1. Reviewed before release

    One-page plain-language guides in English and Spanish, reviewed by a licensed healthcare professional before release.

  2. Measured every session

    We test comprehension before and after every workshop, so we can show people actually learned something.
