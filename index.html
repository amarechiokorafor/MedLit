What the documents actually say

Community Care Pharmacy (281) 555-0142

  1. Rx #4827193

    The number you give the pharmacy when you call to ask for a refill.

  2. DrugLisinopril 10 mg tablets Generic for Zestril

    The medicine, and how much is in each tablet. “Zestril” is a brand name for the very same drug.

  3. SigTake 1 tablet by mouth once daily

    The most important line. One dose every day. Pick a time and keep it the same.

  4. Qty30 tablets

    How many are in the bottle.

  5. Refills2

    You can get it filled twice more before you need a new prescription from your doctor.

  6. Discard after07/24/2027

    Don’t take it after this date without asking your pharmacist first.

Riverside Clinical Lab Collected 07/24/2027

  1. TestHemoglobin A1c

    A blood test that shows your average blood sugar over about the last three months.

  2. Your result6.1 %

    Your number, from the blood drawn that day. One test is one day’s picture. Ask what yours means for you.

  3. Reference range4.0 – 5.6 %

    The span most people fall in. It is a comparison, not a target set for you.

  4. FlagHIGH

    The lab marks anything outside the range automatically. The mark comes from a computer, not from your doctor.

  5. SpecimenBlood, fasting

    What was tested, and how. Fasting means you had not eaten. That changes some numbers.

  6. Ordered byA. Smith, MD

    The clinician who asked for the test. They are who to call with questions.

Meridian Health Plan Explanation of benefits

  1. This isNOT A BILL

    The line people miss. This letter explains what your plan paid. If you owe anything, the clinic sends that separately.

  2. Claim #20270724-88431

    The number to give your plan when you call about this visit.

  3. Date of service07/24/2027

    The day you were seen. Not the day this letter was printed.

  4. Amount billed$482.00

    What the clinic asked for. Almost nobody pays this number.

  5. Plan discount−$310.40

    The amount your plan negotiated away. You do not owe it.

  6. You may owe$34.32

    Wait for the clinic’s bill, then check that it matches this number.

The same instruction, twice

What it says

Take 1 tab PO BID PRN for pain, not to exceed 6 tabs in 24h

What it really means

Take one tablet by mouth twice a day when you have pain. Never more than 6 tablets in one day.

The problem

Every day, people struggle with prescription labels, lab results, and insurance letters. The print is small, the words are hard to understand, and the person who could explain it has already moved on to the next patient.

So people guess. And guessing about your own health is a risk no one should have to take.
