Start a chapter

Run MedLit where you are.

A chapter is a few people in one city who get plain-language health guides onto front desks — a pantry, a clinic waiting room, a library, a senior center — and run a workshop when a room says yes. We send the guides, the kit and the forms. You bring the city.

Free, and free to leave. There are no dues, no quotas, and nothing to sign.

What a chapter actually does.

Less than people expect. A chapter is not a branch office and it does not write the guides — those are written here and reviewed by a licensed healthcare professional before anyone prints them.

How many people
Two is enough to start. One is enough to ask.
How much time
A few hours a month, in practice.
The work
Print guides, ask a front desk, restock it, log what you handed out.
Workshops
Optional. We will run the first one with you over a call if you want it.
What we send
The guides as print-ready files, the starter kit, and the forms below.
What it costs
Nothing. If printing is the thing stopping you, tell us and we will print.
Who can start one
Students, mostly. Nobody has to be pre-med, or a student at all.

Four steps, in order.

  1. 1

    Tell us you want to start one

    The chapter interest form: who you are, what city, and what you have in mind. It takes about three minutes, and it is not an application — we have never said no. Someone reads it and emails you back.

    Chapter interest form — link coming soon In the meantime, email us and say which city. That works just as well.

  2. 2

    Get the starter kit

    One PDF with everything a first month needs: the print settings for the guides, a one-page script for asking a front desk, what to say when they ask who we are, a sheet to leave behind, and the MedLit logo files.

    • Print-ready files for every guide, in all five languages
    • How to ask a front desk, in one page
    • A leave-behind that explains MedLit to a partner
    • The workshop outline, with the timings
    • Logo files and the two things we ask you not to do with them

    Starter kit — link coming soon

  3. 3

    Sign up your own volunteers

    A volunteer form you can share as your own — post it, print the link, put it in a group chat. Everyone who fills it in comes through to us and to you, so you are not retyping names into a spreadsheet.

    Volunteer form to share — link coming soon

  4. 4

    Tell us what you handed out, once a month

    The monthly check-in: where you went, how many guides, which languages. This is the only thing we ask you to keep doing, and it is the reason the numbers on the front page are real. Five minutes. A month with nothing to report is a valid answer.

    Monthly check-in — link coming soon

Not sure yet? Ask anyway.

You do not need a plan, a team, or a partner lined up. If you are thinking about it, email us and say where you are — we will tell you honestly whether a chapter makes sense there and what the first month would look like.
